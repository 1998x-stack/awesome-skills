# Prosocial multiplayer rubric

Use this file for co-op, social worlds, guilds, hubs, gifting, mentoring, matchmaking, and community system reviews.

## Review dimensions

### Shared purpose
- Do players pursue a meaningful common goal?
- Is cooperation intrinsic or merely reward-efficient?
- Are players encouraged to notice each other as people, not just utility nodes?

### Low-risk helpfulness
- Can players help without massive opportunity cost?
- Are there quick supportive actions that feel good immediately?
- Is helping legible to both parties?

### Trust formation
- How does the first positive interaction happen?
- Are players paired into situations where trust can emerge gradually?
- Does the system create repeated encounters, memory, or ritual?

### Consent and safety
- Can players control who can contact, follow, invite, or speak to them?
- Are communication channels unlocked appropriately for age, trust, or context?
- Is the safest path also the easiest path?

### Toxicity pressure
- What mechanics create blame, humiliation, harassment, envy, or spam?
- Does failure cause resentment toward teammates?
- Do rankings, scarcity, or monetization amplify hostility?

### Expressive warmth
- Beyond chat, how can players express care, presence, gratitude, or solidarity?
- Can emotional support be communicated through movement, animation, shared actions, or gifting?

## Output pattern
For each issue, write:
- observed risk
- why it damages the intended community
- safer alternative
- likely cost or tradeoff
