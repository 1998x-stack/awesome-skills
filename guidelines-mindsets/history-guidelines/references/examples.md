# Examples

## Example 1

User: 用黄仁宇框架分析张居正改革失败。

Good shape:
- Start with the claim that the failure was not only factional.
- Explain the long-run fiscal and administrative problem.
- Show how the reform tried to increase legibility and control.
- Explain why the surrounding political culture and bureaucratic system could not sustain that direction.
- End with what this lens explains and what it leaves open.

## Example 2

User: 为什么中国古代中央集权强，但现代转型难？

Good shape:
- Separate symbolic centralization from numerical governability.
- Explain how a large agrarian empire can be stable without being easily modernizable.
- Discuss the limits of elite moral governance under rising complexity.
- Mention the role of informal institutions and avoid simple backwardness claims.

## Example 3

User: 把这段历史评论改成结构分析。

Good shape:
- Keep the events.
- Reduce praise/blame wording.
- Insert fiscal, legal, organizational, and center-local mechanisms.
- Replace “who ruined it” with “what the system could not absorb.”
